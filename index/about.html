I'm a mathematician at Tufts University, working in geometric group theory. My advisors are Genevieve Walsh and Corey Bregman.

I'm currently a third-year Ph.D. candidate, with interests in discrete subgroups of Lie groups and coarse geometry of arbitrary discrete groups. Specific topics I am interested in, in no particular order: lattices in algebraic groups over fields (especially arithmetic lattices), sub-Riemannian geometry of nilpotent groups, unitary representations of discrete groups, non-arithmetic groups in SO(2,1), asymptotic cones of groups, quasi-isometry invariants among classes of groups, and finiteness properties of groups.

In my personal life I enjoy pencil drawing, indoor bouldering, and reading in nature.
